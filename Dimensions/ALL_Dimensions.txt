Note Where To Find Dimensions : 

1) Abstract : Main themes and research areas
2) Literature Review / Background : Existing concepts, theories, and frameworks
3) Theoretical Framework / Conceptual Framework : Previously established dimensions
4) Methodology : How dimensions or categories were identified
5) Results / Findings :	Dimensions and attributes extracted from the literature
6) Discussion : Relationships between dimensions and their importance
7) Tables and Figures : Often contain the actual dimensions and corresponding attributes
