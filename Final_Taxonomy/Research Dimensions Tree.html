1Research Design and Analytical Methods

  • 1.1

    Scientometric Mapping

    Bibliometric and co-citation mapping; Keyword co-occurrence and thematic clustering; Publication trend and research-gap analysis

  • 1.2

    Qualitative Content Analysis

    Literature, narrative and rapid reviews; Semi-structured interviews and focus groups; Thematic coding and saturation; Topic and theme extraction from text (news, social media); Experience reports, commentaries and best-practice synthesis; Comparative analysis of existing systems and frameworks

  • 1.3

    Qualitative Framework

    Conceptual and assessment frameworks (multi-domain service assessment); Supply / processing / distribution management domains; Phased national blood shortage plans (Green, Amber, Red, Recovery); Emergency rationing frameworks; Comparative compatibility and distribution frameworks

  • 1.4

    Statistical and Epidemiological Data Analysis

    Cross-sectional surveys; Retrospective registry and annual-report analysis; Before–after (pre/post) intervention comparisons; Descriptive statistics (frequencies, means, medians, CI); Hypothesis testing (chi-square, ANOVA, Kruskal–Wallis, Wilcoxon, regression); Prevalence, rate and trend analysis

  • 1.5

    Network Analysis Approaches

    Graph-based analysis of actors and links; Collaboration and co-authorship networks; Centrality and connectivity measures

  • 1.6

    Geospatial Analysis and GIS

    GIS mapping and geocoding; Buffer, proximity and select-by-location analysis; Choropleth and census-joined maps; Geographic access and blood deserts; Location-allocation analysis

2Optimization, Modelling and Intelligent Techniques

  • 2.1

    Optimization Approaches

    Mathematical programming (LP, MILP, MINLP); Multi-objective optimization (Pareto solutions); Robust and possibilistic programming; Predict-then-optimize pipelines; Algorithm-driven redistribution and process optimization

  • 2.2

    Quantitative Optimization

    Objective functions (cost, time, shortage, wastage, unmet demand); Solution methods (exact solvers, weighted Tchebycheff, piecewise linear approximation); Routing heuristics (Clarke–Wright savings, 2-opt); Inventory formulas (safety stock, critical and maximum levels); Performance and accuracy metrics (MAE, RMSE, MAPE, R², accuracy)

  • 2.3

    Hybrid Optimization Algorithms / Metaheuristics

    Simulated annealing; Nature-inspired metaheuristics; Integration of mathematical programming with heuristics; Metaheuristic tuning of machine-learning models

  • 2.4

    Simulation and Modeling Approaches

    Time-series models (ARIMA, ETS, SARIMA, Prophet, hierarchical time series); Predictive and regression models; Deterministic systems-flow and analytic models; Scenario simulation; Population-specific distribution modeling

  • 2.5

    Uncertainty Quantification

    Robust and possibilistic uncertainty modelling; Sensitivity analysis; Prediction intervals; External, out-of-sample and cross-validation; Rolling-origin backtesting and model generalizability

  • 2.6

    Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence Techniques

    Neural networks (backpropagation, Kohonen SOM, LSTM); Ensemble and tree-based learners (Random Forest, XGBoost); Classical classifiers (SVM, KNN, decision trees); Deep learning and hybrid ensembles; Topic modelling; Feature engineering, feature selection and explainability

  • 2.7

    Decision Support Approaches

    Decision support tools and dashboards; Prediction-based decision support; Clinical and prehospital transfusion decision support; Location, selection and routing decisions; Expert and committee consensus decision making; Reports and analytics for managers and policymakers

3Blood Supply Chain Planning and Operations

  • 3.1

    Blood Supply Chain Network Design

    Multi-echelon network configuration; Closed-loop and reverse logistics; Facility flows across collection, processing, storage, distribution and disposal

  • 3.2

    Blood Bank Location and Selection Optimization

    Local blood bank and hospital selection; Permanent and temporary collection centre siting; Mobile blood drive site selection

  • 3.3

    Blood Supply and Demand Forecasting

    Demand forecasting (by blood group, product, department, region); Donation and supply forecasting; Blood loss and patient requirement estimation; Utilisation and workload trends; Seasonal, holiday and event-driven patterns

  • 3.4

    Donor Management, Recruitment and Retention

    Donor registration and eligibility; Donor demographics and behaviour; Recruitment campaigns and community engagement; Donor retention and reminders; Donor safety; Mobile and fixed donation sites; Rare and special donor panels

  • 3.5

    Inventory Management and Optimization

    Stock level policies (safety, critical, maximum stock); Centralised and pooled inventory; Real-time inventory tracking and reporting; Issuing policies (FIFO, days on hand); Inventory planning by blood group and product

  • 3.6

    Vehicle Routing and Distribution Optimization

    Vehicle routing and route improvement; Inter-hospital redistribution and transshipment; Transport modes (ground, ambulance, helicopter); Delivery time and cold-chain transport

  • 3.7

    Blood Allocation and Compatibility Frameworks

    ABO/RhD compatibility and identical-group prioritisation; Antigen-negative and rare blood matching; Allocation across hospitals and departments; Rationing and prioritisation during shortage

  • 3.8

    Blood Wastage and Shelf-life Management

    Expiry and outdate wastage; Discard rates; Near-expiry tracking and recirculation; Shelf-life and storage age of components

4Blood Products, Safety and Quality

  • 4.1

    Blood Products and Transfusion Strategies

    Whole blood and low-titre group O whole blood; Plasma types (fresh frozen, liquid, lyophilised, convalescent); Platelets (including cold-stored); Special red cell products (leucoreduced, washed, irradiated, frozen, antigen-negative, paediatric splits); Prehospital transfusion; Apheresis therapies and exchange transfusion

  • 4.2

    Transfusion-Transmissible Infections and Blood Safety Screening

    HIV, HBV, HCV and syphilis; Emerging and vector-borne infections; NAT and ELISA screening; TTI prevalence and trends

  • 4.3

    Quality Assurance and Performance Evaluation

    Quality indicators and performance scoring; External and internal quality assurance; Proficiency testing; SOP compliance and haemovigilance; Staff training and competency; Operational efficiency metrics

5Risk, Resilience and Emergency Management

  • 5.1

    Risk Category

    Supply risks (shortage, donor decline, scarce blood groups); Demand risks (demand surges, demand uncertainty); Operational risks (wastage, manual records, delays, human error); Clinical risks (haemorrhage, incompatibility, alloimmunisation, infection); External risks (disasters, armed conflict, pandemics); Information risks (data breaches, unauthorised access)

  • 5.2

    Risk Management Approach

    Mitigation and contingency strategies; Inventory thresholds, alerts and redistribution; Restrictive transfusion and patient blood management; Backup systems and business continuity; Training, education and process standardisation

  • 5.3

    Emergency Preparedness and Crisis Response

    Armed conflicts; Man-made and natural disasters; Pandemics; Emergency plans and drills; Business continuity; Mass-casualty response

6Digital Health and Information Technology

  • 6.1

    Digital Health Technology

    Alert and notification systems; Disaster management and emergency healthcare applications; Blood donation and health monitoring mobile applications; Web-based blood bank management systems; Cloud platforms; Hospital and transfusion information system integration; GPS-based donor and blood bank locators; Electronic consent and e-signatures

  • 6.2

    IoT and Sensor-based Monitoring

    Sensor nodes and microcontrollers; Cold-chain temperature and humidity monitoring; Real-time stock sensing; Beacon-based donor finding

  • 6.3

    Data Security, Privacy and Integrity

    Authentication; Role-based access control; Encryption; Auditing and integrity verification; Regulatory data-protection compliance

7Governance, Policy, Ethics and Economics

  • 7.1

    Governance, Coordination and Collaboration Networks

    National and international coordination bodies; Inter-organisational collaboration; Command and coordination centres; Policy and strategic planning

  • 7.2

    Ethical, Legal and Regulatory Frameworks

    Regulations and standards; Donor deferral rules; Informed consent; Fair allocation and prioritisation ethics; Clinical trial and regulatory compliance

  • 7.3

    Cost and Economic Evaluation

    Total cost minimisation; Cost savings; Labour cost; Product and programme cost estimation; Economic feasibility

8Application Context

  • 8.1

    Application Domain

    Transfusion haematology; Emergency, prehospital and trauma care; Military and conflict settings; Disaster and humanitarian relief; Hospital and intra-hospital blood management; National and regional blood services; Low- and middle-income country settings; Clinical trials
